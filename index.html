A note on these two pages

The review published here is the public edition. Three findings that named specific unpatched software on a live site that takes card payments have been generalised, because this page can be opened by anyone with the link. Every score, figure, cost and recommendation is unchanged, and the board has the full version. The prototype is a design mockup: nothing on it submits, charges, or connects to anything.
